how 2 ACTUALLY dll hook roblox clients

tutorial by Skyler-sama, thanks stackexchange for the help!!!! heart!

1- install this from archive.org http://www.woodmann.com/collaborative/tools/images/Bin_LordPE_2010-6-29_3.9_LordPE_1.41_Deluxe_b.zip
2- open lordpe and click on "PE Editor" and select ur Roblox exe binary
3- click on directories
4- in the directory information, there's ImportTable. click on it's ellipsis (aka 3 dots)
5- right click on the ImportTable listing and choose "add import"
6- add ur dll's name and an export from ur DLL
7- press ok and save

enjoy your hooked dll! heart!
