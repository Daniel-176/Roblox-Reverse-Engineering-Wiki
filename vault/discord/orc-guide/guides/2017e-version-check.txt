How to bypass "Roblox version is out of date. Please uninstall and try again" on Danills clients

This guide covers 2017E, I have not tested this on anything older / newer than 2017E. 

You need: x32DBG & a copy of RCCService

open x32dbg
open your exe
Click Symbols
open your exe in Symbols
press Az 
search Checking Hash: %s
click Checking Hash: %s
run analyse in the command bar
Scroll up until the red line ends
Find wcstombs+70
You see mov al,1 above it
Right click on mov al,1 and copy its address
click on the je & press space
Replace je with jz and replace the address with the one you copied 
it should look like this
jz 0x00827F17
click ok
