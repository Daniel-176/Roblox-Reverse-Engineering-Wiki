# Legacy Roblox Site Images and Textures

*Credits: original image creators not identified; xs08c provided the Graphictoria texture archives to Vand. Shared in ORC Guide on February 21 and 24, 2026.*

Two image collections from the legacy Roblox website are available in the Vault:

- [2009 site images](/vault/discord/orc-guide/website/images/2009-images.rar): 116 image files in 10 folders. The post describes these as collected from various sources.
- [2011-2021 site images](/vault/discord/orc-guide/website/images/site-images-2011-2021.7z): 660 files in 51 folders, covering 2011 through 2021.

The source posts do not identify the original image authors.
