## ORCdot7z

*Credits: OMEGA DEV team*

Patching guides for Roblox clients and RCCService. The archive was marked incomplete and private in June 2024. No public download link is listed here.

## rblx-scripts

*Credits: repository owner yoshi295295; resource entry by `(ﾟ∀ﾟ)`*

Legacy `/game/` scripts, including join scripts, from 2006-2018. The June 2024 entry marked the repository as taken down and noted missing scripts. Check web archives for copies.

- [GitHub repository](https://github.com/yoshi295295/rblx-scripts)

## Circuitron

[9n8e/Circuitron](https://github.com/9n8e/Circuitron) is a DLL-hook project for legacy Roblox clients.

## k52d/roblox

[k52d/roblox](https://github.com/k52d/roblox) is a fork with 64-bit support and Qt 5. Watrabi reported frequent crashes after x64 builds were added.
