# Roblonium Archive

*Credits: gravity_hq (resource notes). From Roblox Revival Helpers, June 15, 2024.*

*Outlink*

A community archive of Roblox applications, Studio builds, Player clients, and RCCService versions. The 2024 resource entry listed builds from 2007-2018, including versions from test releases. It warned that many files were corrupt and only a few versions per year were fully working.

- **URL:** <https://archive.roblonium.com/>
- **Version and condition notes:** from the 2024 resource entry.
